Our Application Process

A meticulous, multi-stage process ensures proper bonding and maximum longevity.

1

Decontamination

Chemical and mechanical removal of iron particles, tar, and embedded grit.

2

Paint Correction

Machine polishing to remove swirls and scratches, creating a flawless canvas.

3

Panel Prep

Alcohol wipe-down to strip all remaining polishing oils, ensuring bare clear coat.

4

Coating & Curing

Precise application of the chosen coating followed by controlled infrared curing.
