Honest Work. Local Roots.

What started with my father teaching me paint correction in the 1990s has grown into a detailing business built on the same values: honesty, quality and respect for the vehicle.

Our Story

A Family Homestead. A Father's Standards.

My family built our homestead here on the North Shore around 160 years ago, and it's still where the family calls home today. It's the kind of place that teaches you to look after things properly, because the alternative is watching them fall apart.

My father was a national award-winning painter, and back in the 1990s he started teaching me paint correction in the family workshop. Long hours, no shortcuts, and a standard for finish that most people never see up close. Those early lessons stuck. They're still the bar I hold every job to.

Honest Autos grew out of that grounding: a decision to focus on honest, high-quality detailing and ceramic coating work for Orewa and the North Shore, rather than chase volume. Every car that comes through gets the same practical approach: real results, respect for the vehicle's originality (especially with classics), and the kind of relationship that has people coming back for years, not just once.
